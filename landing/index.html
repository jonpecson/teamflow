TeamFlow

Everything your team needs

No bloat. No subscriptions. Just fast, focused communication.

Real-time Messaging

Instant messaging with channels, DMs, link previews, and message history. WebSocket-powered for zero-delay delivery.

Huddle Calls

Slack-style huddles with voice, video, and screen sharing. WebRTC peer-to-peer with AWS Chime SDK signaling.

Desktop Native

Built with Tauri for a lightweight native experience. Backend embedded in the app - no separate server needed.

Self-hosted

Your data stays yours. Shared PostgreSQL for team sync, all compute runs on your machine. No cloud lock-in.

Team Management

Invite codes, channel permissions, online presence, and member management. Built for teams of 2-50.

Session Protection

Auto-cleanup of orphaned calls, rate limiting, max duration caps, and 30-second disconnect detection.
